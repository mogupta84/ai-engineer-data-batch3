==== P2. The question for the temperature test ====
A customer says their order arrived cold and an hour late. In two sentences, what should a support agent say to them?

==== P3a. The question with no documents ====
What is FreshCart's limit on refund claims per customer?

==== P3b. The system message with the policy (paste it into the Instructions box) ====
You answer FreshCart support staff. Use only the policy below.

FreshCart Customer Refund Policy, Version 3.0, effective 01 April 2024, supersedes v2.0 (2022).
1. Scope. This policy governs refunds for food delivery orders placed through the FreshCart consumer application. It
applies to all orders in India from the effective date above. Restaurant partner liability is governed separately by
the Partner Agreement.
2. Eligible reasons for refund. A customer is eligible to request a refund where the order was not delivered, was
delivered materially incomplete, arrived in a spilled or damaged condition, or where the items delivered do not match
the items ordered.
2.1 Spillage and damage. Spillage means leakage of liquid or semi-solid contents from the packaging such that the item
is unfit for consumption. Partial leakage that does not render the item unfit is assessed as damage and attracts a
partial refund of up to fifty percent of item value. Photographic evidence must be submitted within 24 hours of
delivery.
3. Claim frequency limits. A customer may make up to three refund claims in any rolling thirty day period. A fourth
and any subsequent claim within that period is automatically declined by the system and must be escalated to a
support supervisor for manual review. This limit was raised from two claims under v2.0 of this policy.
4. Refund timelines. Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within five to seven working days.
Wallet credit, where the customer elects it, is applied immediately.
5. Exclusions. Refunds are not available where the customer provided an incorrect delivery address, where delivery
was refused without cause, or where the claim is made more than 48 hours after the delivery timestamp.

==== P4c. Few-shot: add these lines at the end of the P4a system message ====

"I run Spice Route on your app. When will last week's settlement reach our account?" is restaurant_partner
"Money left my card twice for one order, please check." is payment
"I was charged a delivery fee on a free delivery order." is payment
